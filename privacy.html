Privacy Policy

PSD Mockup Generator · Effective 21 November 2026

Who uses this app

PSD Mockup Generator is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Drive to read 3D model files and to save the mockup files it creates next to them. It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to create the owner's mockups. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately in the owner's own Google Drive and in secure environment settings that only the owner controls.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
